# Preface

This volume brings together **new research** on the *material
culture* of eating in early modern Europe. Its chapters trace how
prices, wages and diets changed between 1500 and 1800.[^1]

[^1]: See Braudel (1979) for the classic statement of this view.

## The Price of Bread

Bread prices rose faster than wages after 1550, and the economic burden fell on urban households.[^2] The real wage index
is defined as $w_t / p_t$.
